McGraw-Hill Salesforce Misconfiguration

ShinyHunters exploited a misconfigured Salesforce Experience Cloud guest user permission granting unauthenticated access to the /s/sfsites/aura endpoint. No exploit needed — just a misconfigured SaaS. 13.5M records confirmed by HIBP. DIVD issued advisory DIVD-2026-00005 confirming industry-wide issue.
