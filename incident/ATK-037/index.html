Stryker MDM Wiper Attack

Iran-linked Handala group compromised a single Microsoft Intune admin account and remotely wiped 200,000+ devices across 79 countries. No malware — used Stryker's own MDM platform. Manufacturing and shipping halted. FBI seized Handala's infrastructure on March 19.
